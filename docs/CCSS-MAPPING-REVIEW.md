# Separate CCSS mapping review

Reviewed 9 October 2026. EDUCADE IDs identify skills independently of standards.
The supplied coverage images guide the taxonomy, not the CCSS mappings.

The Grade 5 standard identifiers and wording were checked against the RL, RF, W
and L pages at thecorestandards.org. That site identifies itself as an **unofficial
reference mirror**, not the CCSS publisher. The California Department of Education
primary publication returned a network proxy 403. Do not claim primary-source
verification or official endorsement.

Each fictional challenge was separately compared with its stated standard:
- Inference now requires an accurate quotation (RL.5.1).
- Character comparison refers to both characters and their dialogue (RL.5.3).
- Figurative and contextual meanings are separate RL.5.4 skill tasks.
- The reading-foundations sample is a fictional teacher-recorded syllabication
  check, not an automated speech assessment; it practices part of RF.5.3.a.
- Writing examples practice a targeted component, not an entire essay standard.
- Grammar and vocabulary prompts elicit the specific functions described by
  the selected L.5 substandards.

Task alignments remain **candidates / partial standard coverage**. Checked
reference wording is not validation of a rubric, curriculum or mastery score.
